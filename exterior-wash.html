What We Clean

Our full exterior wash covers the entire surface of your truck from cab roof to trailer skirts. We pay special attention to the areas that accumulate the most grime on Houston highways — fuel caps, mud flaps, undercarriage spray zones, and trailer doors. Every wash includes a high-pressure pre-rinse to loosen road debris before detergent application.

Full Exterior Truck Wash — Houston FAQ

How often should Houston fleet operators wash their trucks?

For commercial trucks running regular Houston routes, a full exterior wash every 1–2 weeks is recommended. Houston's humidity, road salt near the coast, and diesel particulates build up quickly and can accelerate paint degradation if left untreated.

Do you wash the trailer as well as the cab?

Yes. Our full exterior wash covers the complete unit — cab, trailer, tires, and all exterior surfaces. We don't do half jobs.

Can you service our trucks at our Houston yard?

That's exactly how we operate. Quick Wash is 100% mobile — we come to your Houston terminal, yard, or parking facility. No appointment required for existing fleet clients on a scheduled contract.

Do you use eco-friendly products?

Yes. All our exterior wash products are biodegradable and responsibly disposed of. We follow eco-conscious water management practices on every job across the Houston area.

How long does a full exterior truck wash take?

A standard full exterior wash on a single unit takes approximately 30–45 minutes. Fleet jobs are scheduled to minimize downtime — we work efficiently so your trucks get back on Houston roads fast.
